Screening rules

Every candidate is checked against these before it is listed.

Austria

  • MRG Altbau (permit before 1945/53) in Vienna falls under Richtwert rent caps, so yield is capped. Price must reflect it.
  • Old unlimited leases (unbefristet, Friedens-/Kategoriezins) can cut value by 40–60%.
  • Baurecht (leasehold), WBF-subsidised units, Genossenschaft/Mietkauf: resale and rent restrictions.
  • Grünland, Kleingarten, Aufschließungsgebiet: little or no building allowed. OÖ Grundverkehr limits farm land.
  • Attic potential only counts with Widmung/Bauklasse headroom; Schutzzone and Denkmalschutz can block it.
  • Wohnrecht / Fruchtgenuss in the Grundbuch, Vorkaufsrecht, HQ100 flood zones, Zweitwohnsitz bans (Salzkammergut).
  • Court auctions (Ediktsdatei): 10% Vadium, no warranty, often no viewing.

Israel

  • דייר מוגן protected tenant: rent fixed by law, near-impossible eviction. Reject unless priced as such.
  • Unregistered rights (חברה משכנת, unregistered condo), RMI leasehold issues: harder financing and resale.
  • Conservation (שימור) limits additions; illegal additions (חריגות בנייה) expose you to demolition orders.
  • Agricultural land "investments": rejected outright.
  • Urban renewal counts only at an advanced stage: signed developer, majority signatures, or approved plan. Tel Aviv replaced TAMA 38 with the Rova'im plans.
  • Betterment levy (היטל השבחה) exposure when using rights; 8% purchase tax on investment units.
  • Receivership sales: bid process, as-is, short deadlines.

Scoring

  • Discount to area €/m² from current benchmarks.
  • Clarity and size of the value-add (extra m², split, renewal stage).
  • Renovation need: cosmetic is fine, structural is penalised. New builds are excluded.
  • Legal limits found, weighted by impact on exit value.
  • Strong 80+, Good 65–79, Watch below 65 with a specific trigger.
